    Rules & odds

    Hold either pig and release to toss both. Hold longer for a harder toss; the odds stay the same. Swipe or drag the empty playing field to rotate all the way around it. A mouse wheel or the arrow keys works too.

    Keep rolling or bank your turn. First to 100 wins. A Pig Out (opposite sides) loses your turn's points. An Oinker (touching pigs) loses your entire game score.
